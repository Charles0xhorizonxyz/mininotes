The pad, at phone size

Open it and write. The arrow in the corner opens the shelves — collections, then books, then notes — where + adds whatever that level holds and ⋮ holds everything about the thing you are standing in. Press and hold any collection, book or note to lift it and drag it into the order you want.

A preview, not the app. HTML standing in for the Android views. On the phone the keyboard opens with the note, the first line of a note is drawn bold because it is that note's name, a long press lifts a line so it can be dragged into place, and the phone's own back button leaves the shelves; here, press and hold lifts in the same way, and Esc stands in for that back button. A web address written in a note is underlined on the phone and opens when it is tapped; here it is only text, because a text box cannot hold a link. The reading size and the paper are both remembered on the phone; here they last until you reload. Sharing records who receives what — nothing is sent yet. Nothing here is stored: reload and the sample pad comes back.
